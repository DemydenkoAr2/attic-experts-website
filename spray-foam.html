What Is It

Insulation That Seals as It Insulates

Spray foam is a liquid insulation that expands on contact and hardens into a solid layer, filling gaps and cavities that rigid materials can't reach. Unlike batt or blown-in insulation, it also acts as an air barrier – so it insulates and seals leaks at the same time.

When You Need It

01

Noticeable drafts or uneven temperatures between rooms

02

High heating/cooling bills despite existing insulation

03

An attic with irregular framing, tight spaces, or lots of penetrations – vents, wiring, plumbing

04

You want a long-term solution and don't plan to touch the attic again for decades
