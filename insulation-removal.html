What Is It

Clean Slate Before New Insulation

The process of extracting old, damaged, or contaminated insulation from an attic or crawl space using vacuum equipment, leaving a clean, safe surface behind — whether you're reinsulating right away or not.

When You Need It

01

Insulation shows signs of rodent droppings, nesting, or odor

02

Visible mold, water damage, or a musty smell in the attic

03

Insulation is compressed, wet, or has lost its effectiveness

04

You're planning a reinsulation project and need a clean base first
